Outside research, I enjoy reading, running, hiking, backpacking, and road trips. I like exploring unfamiliar places, wandering through cities, and spending time in the mountains.

Much like research, these activities encourage me to stay curious, look more closely, and discover something unexpected. I hope to share some of those experiences and moments here.
