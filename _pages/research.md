I develop theoretically grounded and computationally efficient methods at the intersection of generative modeling, graph machine learning, signal processing, and optimization, with applications in complex industrial systems.

**Generative Modeling for Structured Data**

I study how relational structure can inform generative modeling. I develop flow matching methods that incorporate graph structure into source distributions and probability paths, connecting generative learning with graph signal processing. These works were developed in collaboration with [Jhony H. Giraldo](https://jhonygiraldo.github.io/), [Aref Einizade](https://arefeinizade.github.io/), [Wenbin Wang](https://wenbinwang12.github.io/), and [Olga Fink](https://people.epfl.ch/olga.fink?lang=en).

**Optimization-Induced Graph Neural Networks**

I develop optimization-induced and algorithm-informed graph neural networks that adapt to noisy graphs and distribution shifts. These works were developed in collaboration with [Ziping Zhao](https://faculty.sist.shanghaitech.edu.cn/zhao/), [Zengfeng Huang](https://zengfenghuang.github.io/), [Songtao Lu](https://songtaogithub.github.io/), and [Olga Fink](https://people.epfl.ch/olga.fink?lang=en).

**Optimization and Algorithm Unrolling**

I study the connections among minorization-maximization, fractional programming, and gradient-based methods to develop efficient algorithms for nonconvex optimization. I also develop unrolled networks that learn adaptive updates for beamforming and resource allocation. These works were developed in collaboration with [ZheXian Yang](https://openreview.net/profile?id=%7EZhexian_Yang1), [Ziping Zhao](https://faculty.sist.shanghaitech.edu.cn/zhao/), [Kaiming Shen](https://kaimingshen.github.io/), [Daniel P. Palomar](https://www.danielppalomar.com/), and [Wei Yu](https://www.comm.utoronto.ca/~weiyu/).
